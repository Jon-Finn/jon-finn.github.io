Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

ClipRus is a personal tool that its owner runs on their own computer to post gaming clips to the ClipRus-GTAVI YouTube channel. It is not a service other people sign up for.

Google account data ClipRus uses

When the channel owner connects their YouTube account, ClipRus asks Google for two permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the clips the owner approves.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read view, like and comment counts for videos ClipRus uploaded, so it can learn which clips do well.

ClipRus does not read, change or delete any other videos, messages, contacts or personal information.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token and the view counts are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. ClipRus has no server and no database of its own, and it does not send this data anywhere except to Google's YouTube API.

Sharing

ClipRus does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising. ClipRus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes ClipRus's access.
