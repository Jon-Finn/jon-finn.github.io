ClipRus

The best moments from gaming livestreams, cut into short vertical videos.

What ClipRus is

ClipRus runs the ClipRus-GTAVI channel on YouTube. It looks for popular clips of Grand Theft Auto streams on Twitch, reformats them for phones with captions, and posts them as Shorts. Every clip credits the streamer and links to their channel.

How it works

  • Finds clips that are getting a lot of views on Twitch.
  • Turns each one into a 9:16 video with captions and a credit to the streamer.
  • A person reviews every clip before it is posted.
  • Posts approved clips to the ClipRus channel on a schedule.

For streamers

We only clip streamers who allow it. If you'd like your clips added, or removed, email jon-finn@hotmail.com and we'll act on it within 48 hours.
